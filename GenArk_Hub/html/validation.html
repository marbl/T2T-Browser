Description

Validation of T2T-CHM13v2.0 with HG002Y assembly by various methods

This track grouping contains three tracks annotating known issues and HiFi and ONT read coverage across the T2T-CHM13v2.0 assembly.

Methods

For known issues, annotations display regions with low coverage support and location of each error k-mer obtained from HiFi and ONT platforms.

Reads were aligned with Winnowmap 2.01.

Primary read alignments have been processed with asset commit ver. 0133f268eebf308a1c3eb356b564550526465157, and regions meeting the following criteria are reported in each platform:

  • Less than 7 reads aligned in hifi, 10 reads aligned in ONT
  • Merged when regions are less than 5 kb apart
  • Exclude low coverage at both 3 kb ends of each chromosome due to natural coverage drop
  • Exclude low coverage overlapping rDNA gaps

Additionally, regions below were included:

  • Regions with base error were detected with 21-mers from Illumina-HiFi reads using Merqury
  • Chimeric consensus of two haplotypes and collapsed simple tandem repeat, found from variant calling and other methods

Raw low coverage tracks from each platform (containing clippings and low coverage from sequencing biases):

  • hifi.pri.issues.bed
  • ont.pri.len1k_idy85.issues.bed

For read coverage, reads were aligned as follows:

Input reads: HiFi and ONT reads (guppy 6.3.7~6.3.8)
Mapper: Winnowmap v2.01 release (--MD -W repetitive_k15.txt -ax [map-pb | map-ont])
Filter: Primary read alignments extracted with samtools view -F0x104

Display Conventions and Configuration

Known issues

  •     - Error_Kmer : K-mers found only in the assembly, and not in the Illumina-HiFi hybrid kmer set
  •     - Chimeric_Hap (Joined two haplotypes) or Collapse
  •     - Low (Low coverage) or Low_Qual : Low coverage associated with consensus error
  •     - Inversion breakpoint error at P5 palindrome and a likely assembly error in the Y chromosome

Coverage

The HiFi and ONT coverage containers exclude chromosome Y from their coverage calculations; neither primary nor marker-assisted coverage is provided for chromosome Y.

  •     - HiFi primary alignments (light pink)
  •     - HiFi marker-assisted alignments (light coral)
  •     - ONT primary alignments (light blue)
  •     - ONT marker-assisted alignments (cornflower blue)

ONT primary alignments were further filtered for alignment block lengths > 1 kb and > 85% identity.
