Description

This annotation of the subtelomeres consists of the identification and organization of the subtelomere repeat elements (SREs). The SREs are subtelomeric DNA segmental duplications which are present in the most distal 500kb human DNA. They contain mosaic patchworks of duplicons, namely the paralogy blocks (pblocks). Previously defined subtelomere-representative pblocks (Stong et al. 2014) covered most SREs, but we identified 11 new pblocks in hg002. The annotation is carried out by comparing the existing representative pblocks with the hg002 reference and new pblocks are added based on the newly discovered SRE segments in hg002.

Methods

Representative sequences for existing pblocks were masked (for simple and tandem repeats) and then aligned to the reference sequence, requiring a minimum of 90% identity and 100 bp alignment length. Groups of alignments (localized, ordered, co-directional, greater than 1kb chain- length) form a mapping location of a pblock to the reference. If a pblock aligned only partially with a given segment of the reference sequence, then if possible, it was extended by pairwise alignment of the unmasked p-block sequence to the flanking segment(s) of the reference. If neighboring pblocks overlapped, then the one with the highest percent identity to the reference in the overlapping area was selected. Post-processing: After all pblocks were mapped, we investigated for potential new pblocks: (i) gaps greater than 1kb between existing mapped pblocks within a subtelomere, (ii) the subtelomere reference areas towards the centromeric side of the subtelomere region (< 500kb), where no existing pblock mapped. The investigation was carried out by masking (for simple and tandem repeats) these candidate pblock regions and aligning them to the reference. If a segment of a subtelomere mapped to multiple subtelomere areas (< 500 kb from telomeres), it was considered a valid new subtelomeric pblock.

Display Conventions and Configuration

The track shows the locations of the pblocks and their percent identity with the reference. Each pblock has a unique color.
